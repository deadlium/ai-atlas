# AI Atlas Catalog Licensing & Content Policy

This document clarifies the intellectual property, content licensing, and trademark policies governing the **AI Atlas** catalog, its metadata, and curated descriptions.

---

## 1. Scope and Separation of Rights

AI Atlas is a dual-licensed project separating software source code from curated catalog data and third-party references.

```
AI Atlas Project
├── Software Code & Tooling  ───> MIT License (see LICENSE)
├── Original Catalog Content ───> Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
└── Third-Party Assets       ───> Retained by their respective owners / upstream licenses
```

### 1.1 Software and Automation Code
All original code created for the AI Atlas repository (including validation scripts, data parsers, build tooling, web applications, and schema definitions) is released under the **[MIT License](LICENSE)**.

### 1.2 Original Catalog Content and Curated Metadata
All original catalog descriptions, curation notes, category taxonomies, and synthesized metadata authored by AI Atlas contributors are made available under the **[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**.

Under CC BY 4.0, you are free to:
- **Share**: Copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format.
- **Adapt**: Remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, even commercially.

Under the following terms:
- **Attribution**: You must give appropriate credit to the AI Atlas project, provide a link to the license, and indicate if changes were made.

## 2. Third-Party Resources, Software, and Trademarks

AI Atlas indexes and references numerous external tools, models, frameworks, SDKs, datasets, and platforms. Listing these resources does **not** grant AI Atlas any ownership over them, nor does AI Atlas claim the right to relicense external intellectual property.

### 2.1 Third-Party Source Code & Artifacts
- Source code, weights, model files, and packages of cataloged resources remain exclusively subject to their respective upstream licenses (e.g., Apache 2.0, MIT, BSD, GPL, OpenRAIL, proprietary commercial licenses, or custom terms of use).
- Users who download, install, or interface with cataloged tools must comply with the upstream license specified by that resource's authors.

### 2.2 Third-Party Trademarks, Brand Names, and Logos
- All product names, logos, service marks, and company trademarks cited within AI Atlas are the property of their respective trademark holders.
- The use of any trademark or trade name within AI Atlas is strictly nominative and descriptive, intended solely to identify the resource and facilitate discovery.
- Use within AI Atlas does not imply sponsorship, endorsement, affiliation, or certification by the respective trademark owners.

### 2.3 Third-Party Descriptions and Copyrighted Material
- AI Atlas prioritizes **original, factual, and concise descriptions** authored by contributors.
- Direct excerpts, short quotations, or factual summaries from official project documentation are included under fair use principles solely for identification, educational, and categorization purposes.
- AI Atlas does not claim copyright over external documentation, product descriptions, or trademarked taglines.

## 3. Contributor Content Commitment

When contributing catalog entries or descriptions to AI Atlas, contributors represent and agree that:
1. **Originality**: Curated summaries, reviews, and category descriptions are either authored by the contributor or derived from factual, publicly available information.
2. **License Grant**: Any original descriptive text and metadata contributed is licensed to the community under the **CC BY 4.0** license.
3. **No Infringing Content**: Contributions must not include proprietary code, confidential information, unauthorized full-text documentation scrapes, or copyrighted materials without explicit authorization.

---

## 4. Takedown and Inaccuracy Notification

If you are a copyright owner, trademark holder, or project maintainer and believe that any catalog entry infringes your intellectual property or misrepresents your project:

1. Open a GitHub Issue using our **[Report Inaccuracy / Resource Issue](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/report-resource.yml)** template, or
2. Contact the maintainers directly as outlined in our **[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)** or repository issue tracker.

We will review reports promptly and make appropriate corrections or removals.
